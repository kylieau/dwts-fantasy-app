Mirrorball Madness — screen concepts, v3

Chrome that only shows up when it's earned

Thirteen screens. The four in-league tabs (Home, Your picks, This week, Standings) never change — they're the only permanent navigation. Cross-league browsing lives behind a switcher you open from Home, not a competing tab, and only matters once someone's in more than one league. Zero-league, one-league and multi-league starts are shown separately, along with where account, league and admin settings each live.
